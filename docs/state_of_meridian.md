# State of Meridian (Oct 2019 - Aug 2020 vs the year before)

## The answer
Meridian sold 12% fewer items (15.52M -> 13.64M). This is NOT mainly COVID: sales were already falling 11% before lockdown.

## Why (3 reasons)

### 1. Customers buy less and come back less
- Customers -4.7%, visits per customer -2.7%, items per visit -4.6%. Smaller baskets are the biggest driver.
- Only 12-20% of new customers buy again the next month.
- From Aug 2019, every customer group (old and new) started coming back less often.

### 2. Everyday tops and trousers are failing
- Tops and trousers = ~80% of all lost items, and they were already down ~15% before COVID.
- T-shirts fell twice as fast as other tops (-31%). The main reason: 20% fewer T-shirt designs on sale (3,751 -> 2,995).
- Dresses were growing (+13%) until COVID. They are the only real COVID victim.

### 3. Online, our biggest channel, is weakening
- Online = ~70% of items, and it fell faster than stores before COVID (-12% vs -9%).
- With every store closed in lockdown, online still fell 7%.

## What to do on Monday
1. Restore the T-shirt and blouse range. Check with buying why designs were cut.
2. Investigate online: where shoppers drop off and why baskets are smaller (test fixes with A/B tests).
3. Start a win-back programme: 5 in 6 new customers never return. Find who is about to leave (churn model).

## Caveats
- Prices are a scaled index, so this page uses items sold, not revenue or profit.
- No stock data: we can't fully prove T-shirt designs were removed, only that fewer sold.
- Data starts 20 Sep 2018, so early 'new customers' include old regulars.
